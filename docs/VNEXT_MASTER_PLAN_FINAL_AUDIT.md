## Twenty-step end-to-end capability

The complete episode separates decision-time and later truth. Issuance covers
market detection through realistic shadow execution (steps 1–13). Completion
attaches verified settlement, grades every participant, proposes trust updates,
persists the full episode, replays bounded improvements on held-out evidence,
and presents a non-applying promotion candidate (steps 14–20). It does not
rerun the original organism after settlement.

The parser rejects incomplete steps, non-retired temporary agents, relabeled
simulation, broker contact, incumbent substitution, or automatic/applied
promotion. This validates the architecture end to end without granting order,
capital, credential, or promotion authority.

advantage.

Claims 7–8 are `SUPPORTED_GOVERNANCE_ONLY`. They establish execution-truth
separation and fail-closed authority/credential boundaries. They do not prove
execution-model accuracy, forecast skill, profitability, or readiness.

The aggregate review satisfies replay determinism only. The other 12 promotion
evidence fields remain false, so `SHADOW_ONLY` to `REPLAY_VALIDATED` is blocked.
Even a fully satisfied packet could only request human review; it could not
apply promotion automatically.
